FlowTrace: a replay of the backend tracking 4 tags

Simulated readings. No FlowTrace node has been flashed or installed anywhere, and no venue uses this. Four scripted tags walked a 10 m × 10 m floor plan; their BLE signal strengths were generated by scripts/simulate.py's model. What you see is the real backend's output for those readings (/ws positions and /api/v1/analytics/summary), recorded with demo/tools/record_replay.py.

Filled dot: where the backend placed the tag. Hollow ring: where the script actually had it. Squares: the 9 scanner nodes.

Zones and node positions come from the backend's config (VENUE_ZONES, SEED_NODES), written into data/replay.json by the recorder. Bounce = share of finished zone visits under 60 s. A/B = visit share of the two most-visited zones; the backend counts devices that visited a zone, not repeat visits, so with 4 tags it reads 50/50. Source: labs/Flow Trace, see LEARNINGS.md.
