# FlowTrace: The Invisible Engine for Venue Analytics & Growth

**Turn Foot Traffic into Actionable Growth—No Apps, No Friction.**

Welcome to FlowTrace, the first end-to-end passive tracking and agentic analytics platform designed specifically for physical retail spaces, malls, and entertainment zones in Bangladesh (like Bashundhara Group / Togi Fun World). 

## Why FlowTrace?

Traditional analytics require visitors to download apps or log into Wi-Fi portals. FlowTrace changes the game with **Invisible Indoor BLE Tracking**. We capture high-fidelity visitor movement data passively, securely, and seamlessly.

But data without direction is just noise. That's where our **Agentic Analytics Engine** comes in. FlowTrace doesn't just give you a dashboard—it gives you an AI agent that analyzes the data and provides actionable recommendations to optimize operations, boost sales, and enhance visitor experience.

### Core Features

#### 📡 Passive Tracking (Zero Friction)
No apps to download. No QR codes to scan. FlowTrace's advanced BLE sensors map customer journeys passively. Understand dwell times, popular zones, and bottleneck areas with 100% frictionless tracking.

#### 🧠 Agentic AI Insights
Tired of staring at charts? FlowTrace's AI acts as your dedicated business strategist. It automatically spots anomalies, predicts peak times, and recommends layout changes or targeted promotions based on real-time footfall patterns.

#### 📈 High ROI for Venue Owners
- **Optimize Layouts:** Discover underutilized spaces and high-traffic zones to adjust tenant pricing or stall placements.
- **Dynamic Staffing:** Let our AI predict busy periods so you deploy staff exactly when and where they're needed.
- **Boost Tenant Sales:** Provide your tenants with granular data on pass-by traffic versus walk-ins, helping them optimize their storefronts.
