The Current Challenge

Blind Spots in a 70-Ride Operation

Currently, staff manually manage coins and log rides across three floors. Customers buy tickets upfront and wait for attendants to start the ride.

  • No Customer Data: You don't know who is riding, their age, or their preferences.
  • High Friction: Guests wait in line to buy tickets, then wait for staff to process coins.
  • Missed Revenue: No way to retarget customers or upsell rides they haven't tried.

The FlowTrace Solution

Tap, Ride, and Analyze

We replace the manual coin system with smart RFID cards and custom software, turning your Playzone into an intelligent, data-gathering ecosystem.

  • Zero-Friction Entry: Guests grab a card, walk in, and simply tap at any of the 70 rides to play.
  • Smart Checkout: Pay at the end based on exact usage.
  • Automated Data Collection: Every tap logs exactly which rides are used, when, and by whom.
